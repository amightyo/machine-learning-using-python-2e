# Suggested 7-Week Graduate Course

| Week | Focus | Chapters | Major Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations: ML, Python, statistics | 1–3 | Readiness + foundations lab |
| 2 | Problem formulation and data preparation | 4–5 | Framing and leakage audit |
| 3 | Regression and classification | 6–7 | Supervised-learning lab |
| 4 | Trees, SVM, ensembles | 8–10 | Comparative modeling |
| 5 | Unsupervised learning | 11–12 | Clustering/representation lab |
| 6 | Neural networks, NLP, generative AI | 13–15 | Modern-ML investigation |
| 7 | Evaluation, responsibility, reproducibility | 16–18 | Research artifact showcase |

Recommended assessment: knowledge checks, hands-on labs, comparative modeling, responsible-ML audit, and a final reproducible research artifact.
