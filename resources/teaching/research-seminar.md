# Suggested Research Seminar

For a research seminar, Chapters 16–18 form the methodological spine. Earlier chapters become a method library selected according to the research question.

Suggested themes: research contribution; construct validity; provenance and leakage; algorithm choice; calibration and uncertainty; external validation; responsible ML; reproducibility and preregistration; replication; proposal studio; peer review; reproducibility audit; publication positioning; research showcase.

Core deliverable: a literature-grounded, reproducible research artifact with defensible evaluation, responsible-ML analysis, limitations, Quarto manuscript, version-controlled repository, and archival plan.
