# Self-Study Guide

**Stage 1 — Foundations:** Chapters 1–5. Milestone: formulate an ML problem and build a leakage-safe baseline workflow.

**Stage 2 — Core Models:** Chapters 6–12. Milestone: compare model families and justify evaluation.

**Stage 3 — Modern ML:** Chapters 13–15. Milestone: implement or critically evaluate a modern-ML workflow.

**Stage 4 — Evidence and Responsibility:** Chapters 16–17. Milestone: perform an evaluation and responsible-ML audit.

**Stage 5 — Research Artifact:** Chapter 18. Milestone: convert an analysis into a Quarto-based, version-controlled research compendium.

Do not measure progress only by chapters completed. Measure it by questions you can answer and artifacts you can independently produce.
