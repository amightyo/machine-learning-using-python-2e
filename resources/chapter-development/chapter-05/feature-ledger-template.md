## Feature admission questions
1. What does this variable represent?
2. Would it exist at prediction time?
3. How was it measured?
4. What does missing mean?
5. Could it reveal the target indirectly?
6. Does its transformation learn from data?
7. Can the transformation be reproduced?
8. Why should it help solve the specified problem?
