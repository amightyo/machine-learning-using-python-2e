# Algorithmic Impact Assessment

## 1. Purpose
What decision will the system influence, and why is ML appropriate?

## 2. Stakeholders
Who benefits, who is affected, and who bears error costs?

## 3. Alternatives
What non-ML or less automated alternatives exist?

## 4. Harm Analysis
For each plausible harm, record severity, likelihood, affected population, mitigation, and residual risk.

## 5. Evidence
What validation evidence supports the proposed use?

## 6. Fairness
Which fairness concepts matter and why?

## 7. Privacy
What information is collected, inferred, retained, or shared?

## 8. Human Oversight
Who can override the system and under what conditions?

## 9. Contestability
How can an affected person correct data or appeal a decision?

## 10. Monitoring
What performance, fairness, drift, safety, and incident indicators will be monitored?

## 11. Stop Conditions
What findings trigger rollback, suspension, retraining, or retirement?

## 12. Approval
Who is accountable for the deployment decision?
