# Chapter 1 Extended Exercise Bank

## Health application
Using NHANES, identify the unit of analysis, target, candidate feature families, leakage risks, baseline, evaluation metrics, generalization concern, responsibility concern, and one causal claim the predictive model cannot establish.

## Education application
Using IPEDS, define an institution-year prediction problem, prediction horizon, target, pre-outcome features, temporal validation strategy, baseline, sector-comparison issue, and possible misuse.

## Professor's Challenge variants
1. A classifier has 95% accuracy when 95% of observations are in one class. Evaluate it.
2. A retention model uses information recorded after the outcome. Diagnose the problem.
3. A complex ensemble improves AUC from .812 to .816 over logistic regression. What evidence determines whether the gain matters?
4. Financial aid is an important predictor of retention. Can you infer that increasing aid will cause retention to improve?

## Research ladder
Take one public-data idea through reproduction, extension, rigorous benchmarking, methodological contribution, and substantive contribution. For each level state the research question, evidence required, novelty, validation strategy, and limitations.
