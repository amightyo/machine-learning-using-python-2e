# Chapter 10 Crosswalk

Chapter 10 completes the core supervised-learning sequence by generalizing the ensemble idea introduced with Random Forests.

## Technical progression

- ensemble averaging and correlated error;
- bagging recap;
- AdaBoost and weighted difficult cases;
- gradient boosting as functional gradient descent;
- pseudo-residuals;
- learning-rate/ensemble-size tradeoff;
- histogram gradient boosting;
- voting;
- stacking;
- out-of-fold meta-features;
- ensemble diversity;
- calibration;
- shared failure modes.

## Connections

- Chapter 3: bias-variance and optimization;
- Chapter 7: discrimination and calibration;
- Chapter 8: bagging and Random Forests;
- Chapter 9: model-family comparison;
- Chapter 16: rigorous tuning, nested evaluation, uncertainty, and final test separation;
- Chapter 17: responsible ML, subgroup performance, and shared bias.
