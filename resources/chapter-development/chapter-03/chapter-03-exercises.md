# Chapter 3 Extended Exercise Bank

## Descriptive Statistics
Construct two arrays with the same mean but different variance. Visualize them and explain why the mean alone is insufficient.

## Sampling Simulation
Simulate repeated samples for n = 25, 100, and 400. Compare the distributions of sample means and explain the effect of sample size.

## Scaling Experiment
Multiply one feature of a classification dataset by 10,000. Compare k-nearest-neighbor performance before and after scaling.

## Distance Exercise
Compute Euclidean and Manhattan distances between the same observations. Discuss how changing the metric changes the concept of similarity.

## Optimization Experiment
Run the gradient-descent example with several learning rates. Identify slow convergence, useful convergence, oscillation, and divergence.

## Research Reflection
Choose one variable from NHANES or IPEDS. Explain what is lost when the real-world construct is represented by that single numeric variable.

## AI Audit
Ask AI to explain why correlation proves a feature is important. Identify every conceptual error and rewrite the explanation.
