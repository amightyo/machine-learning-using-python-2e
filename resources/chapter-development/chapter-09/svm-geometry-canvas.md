## Representation questions

1. What geometry does the current feature representation impose?
2. Why should observations considered “similar” by the kernel actually be useful for this problem?
3. Is nonlinear flexibility improving generalization or memorizing local structure?
4. Does the application need ranking, hard decisions, or calibrated probabilities?
5. What scientific meaning, if any, can legitimately be attached to the learned boundary?
