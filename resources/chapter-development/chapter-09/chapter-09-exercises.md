# Chapter 9 Extended Exercise Bank

## Geometry by Hand
Given several two-dimensional points and a proposed hyperplane, calculate signed scores and determine predicted classes.

## Margin Experiment
Generate separable data and compare several separating lines. Identify which has the largest visual margin.

## C Experiment
Fit linear SVMs over a grid of C values. Track training accuracy, validation accuracy, margin behavior, and support-vector count.

## Scaling Failure
Multiply one feature by 10,000 and compare SVM behavior before and after standardization.

## Kernel Experiment
Use circles or moons data to compare linear, polynomial, and RBF kernels.

## Gamma Experiment
Hold C fixed while varying gamma from very small to very large. Explain underfitting and overfitting geometrically.

## Probability Audit
Compare decision scores with calibrated probabilities. Explain why they are different quantities.

## Research Interpretation
Ask AI to claim that an RBF kernel discovered the true nonlinear mechanism. Rewrite the claim in scientifically defensible language.
