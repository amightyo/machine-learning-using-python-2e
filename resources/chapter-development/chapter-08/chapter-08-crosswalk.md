# Chapter 8 Crosswalk

Chapter 8 expands tree-based learning into a technical treatment of recursive partitioning, Gini impurity, entropy, split improvement, regression trees, overfitting, structural regularization, cost-complexity pruning, instability, bagging, Random Forests, OOB evaluation, impurity importance, permutation importance, and partial dependence.

The chapter deliberately connects Chapter 3's bias–variance concepts and Chapter 7's classification evaluation to nonlinear tree models.

Boosting is deferred to Chapter 10. Detailed hyperparameter search and nested evaluation remain in Chapter 16. Responsible/fairness analysis returns in Chapter 17.
